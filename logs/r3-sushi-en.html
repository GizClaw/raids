【Narrator】It is the year 1047, in Meishan, a small town in the western hills of Sichuan. In a courtyard shaded by bamboo, a twelve-year-old boy named Su Shi is digging in the soft earth with a wooden spade, while his younger brother Su Zhe kneels beside him.
【Su Shi】I am Su Shi, also called Zizhan. Today I will walk you through my whole life, from a strange stone dug up in this courtyard all the way to the sound of students reading on an island in the southern sea. This is my brother Su Zhe, and he will stay with me for the early part of the journey.

【Su Shi】Look at this! It is a stone fish, as if the river left it here for us to find.
【Su Zhe】Let me wash the mud off. See, it is smooth and cool, and one side is flat, like a small table.
【Narrator】Su Shi turns the stone over in his hands. The flat side feels just right for grinding ink, but the fish shape is so lovely that he does not want to mark it up.
【Su Shi】Brother, I have two thoughts. I could keep it on my desk as a treasure, and never use it. Or I could try grinding ink on it, and see if it writes like a proper inkstone.
【Su Zhe】If we use it, we may scratch its pretty scales. But if it can write, it would be a friend to your poems.
【Narrator】The stone fish lies in Su Shi's palm, half clean and half dusty, waiting to be chosen. Do you think he should keep it as a treasure, or try it as an inkstone?

【Su Shi】A fish of green stone, dug from the yard, no river gave it life, yet it holds the black tide of ink. When my brush moves, it swims across the page, carrying words far beyond these bamboo gates.
【Su Zhe】That is a good first poem. It says more about your heart than about the stone.
【Narrator】The years pass quickly. It is now 1057, and the two brothers are grown young men. Their father has decided that they must go to the capital Bianjing to take the imperial examination, the great test that chooses scholars for the emperor's service.

【Narrator】The road from Meishan to Bianjing is long. They travel by river boat when they can, and by cart when the land rises. Su Shi keeps the fish stone wrapped in cloth inside his box, and Su Zhe keeps their papers in order.
【Su Shi】I have never seen so many people, so many carts, and so many gates as in Bianjing. The city is loud, and the examination hall is quiet.
【Su Zhe】We have studied for this day. Let us write what we truly believe, and not what we think the judges want to hear.
【Narrator】Inside the examination hall, each candidate sits at a small wooden desk. Su Shi unrolls his paper, dips his brush, and begins to write about good government, courage, and the duty of a scholar to speak honestly.

【Ouyang Xiu】This young man writes with a clear and fearless voice. I must step aside and let him take his place. In thirty years, everyone will speak of him.

【Su Zhe】We have only just begun. Whatever comes next, we will face it together.
